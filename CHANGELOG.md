## [1.2.2] - 2026-10-25

### Added
- A girl kept from her letters (before the printing age, in the oldest times even in a scribe's house) can try to learn in secret: half of them do, half are caught.

### Fixed
- A great-grandchild of someone who could read, or a child of scribes, clergy or clerks, is no longer "the first in the family" to read.
- 51 more lines about olives, vineyards, herds, harvest and the plough no longer come to a life that has moved to a town or a port.

## [1.2.1] - 2026-10-25

### Changed
- A life behind the monastery gate now reads like one: 113 lines and five forks of the cloister (novices, copying, bells, meditation, plague in the house, a fugitive at the gate, a year of silence), and no more dances, weddings or village fairs. Hard times, the legacy and the last wish speak of the monastery too.

### Fixed
- A life that went to the sea (a day trip by train, fishing, a house by the shore) is no longer mourned as one that never saw it.
- A childless death is no longer "with the children near".

## [1.1.2] - 2026-10-25

### Fixed
- A widow or widower no longer meets lines about a living spouse; remarrying lifts it.
- Work at a factory, an office or a warehouse makes the life a town life, so fields and rent stop coming.
- After emigrating, a life gets only lines, forks and events true anywhere, not those of the home village.
- Lines about your own children and grandchildren come only to those who have them; three forks ask for what they assumed.

<!-- tg
«Жребий» 1.1.2: жизни стали связнее — вдова больше не встречает строк о живом муже, а после переезда в город или за океан не выпадает деревенское
Строки о своих детях и внуках теперь только у тех, у кого они есть
-->

## [1.1.1] - 2026-10-25

### Fixed
- Entries written on one page now appear one after another, not all at once.
- The screens no longer scroll under the status bar.
- A fork ignores taps for a moment after it appears, so a quick second tap on "Turn the page" can't pick its first door.
- A life that has moved to town no longer gets lines and forks about fields, harvests, cattle or rent: lines about a new flat or a factory hostel mark the life as a town life.
- A father no longer shares his child's name.
- The demo writes its notebook answers in the app's language.

<!-- tg
«Жребий» 1.1.1: записи в книге теперь пишутся по очереди, а экран больше не заезжает под шторку
Кто переехал в город, тому больше не выпадают поле, урожай и аренда участка
Развилку нельзя выбрать случайным вторым касанием «Дальше»

## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-25

### Added
- Ten times the life: about 2,000 everyday lines instead of 200, tied to region, family, sex, letters, marriage and years, so China reads like China and the kolkhoz never comes before 1930.
- 465 real events instead of 72, among them 49 encounters with people later written about.
- Everyday forks: about 150 dilemmas at 19, 27, 39, 51 and 64, with doors shut for some, weighted outcomes, epitaph lines and questions of their own.
- Longer books: more pages per life, from infancy to a hundred.
- A choice before the draw, all optional: an era, a part of the world, born a boy or a girl. Within it the lot keeps the real weights; combinations that never existed are greyed out.
- The chronicle and the encounters in the atlas: what your lives have met so far, era by era.
- New lives avoid lines, events and forks met in your recent books, and events no life has seen yet come first.
- Thirty regional things for the chest, more names, more stories, epitaphs and questions.

### Changed
- "Which one, the lot decides" instead of "the sack decides".
- Finished books are kept as they were written, so later updates of the lines never change them.

<!-- tg
«Жребий» 1.1: жизнь стала в десять раз богаче — около 2000 строк быта, 465 настоящих событий и полторы сотни житейских развилок
Перед жребием теперь можно выбрать эпоху, часть света и пол — внутри выбранного веса остаются честными
В атласе появились «Летопись» и «Встречи» — коллекции событий и знаменитостей, которых застали твои жизни

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-25

### Added
- The sack and the wooden tag: a fair draw from the 117 billion people ever born, weighted by era, region and family, with the odds.
- The parish register: a life read page by page, with about 200 everyday lines across six ages and about 70 real historical events, including rare encounters.
- Forks with every door shown, the shut ones with the reason; witness mode.
- The chest of things; great-grandchildren who inherit the name, the means and the thing.
- A question for you at the end of each life, kept in a notebook; the atlas with a time slider, the census and the encounters.
- An archive of every life, replayed from its seed; EN and RU; demo build.

<!-- tg
Новое приложение — «Жребий»: мешок честно вытягивает тебе одного из 117 миллиардов людей, когда-либо живших на Земле
Жизнь пишется в метрическую книгу, на развилках видны и закрытые двери — с причиной, почему они закрыты
Каждая жизнь оставляет вещь в сундуке, правнук может её унаследовать, а в конце жизнь задаёт тебе один вопрос
