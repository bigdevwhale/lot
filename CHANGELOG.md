## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-25

### Added
- Ten times the life: about 2,000 everyday lines instead of 200, tied to region, family, sex, letters, marriage and years, so China reads like China and the kolkhoz never comes before 1930.
- 465 real events instead of 72, among them 49 encounters with people later written about.
- Everyday forks: about 150 dilemmas at 19, 27, 39, 51 and 64, with doors shut for some, weighted outcomes, epitaph lines and questions of their own.
- Longer books: more pages per life, from infancy to a hundred.
- A choice before the draw, all optional: an era, a part of the world, born a boy or a girl. Within it the lot keeps the real weights; combinations that never existed are greyed out.
- The chronicle and the encounters in the atlas: what your lives have met so far, era by era.
- New lives avoid lines, events and forks met in your recent books, and events no life has seen yet come first.
- Thirty regional things for the chest, more names, more stories, epitaphs and questions.

### Changed
- "Which one, the lot decides" instead of "the sack decides".
- Finished books are kept as they were written, so later updates of the lines never change them.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-25

### Added
- The sack and the wooden tag: a fair draw from the 117 billion people ever born, weighted by era, region and family, with the odds.
- The parish register: a life read page by page, with about 200 everyday lines across six ages and about 70 real historical events, including rare encounters.
- Forks with every door shown, the shut ones with the reason; witness mode.
- The chest of things; great-grandchildren who inherit the name, the means and the thing.
- A question for you at the end of each life, kept in a notebook; the atlas with a time slider, the census and the encounters.
- An archive of every life, replayed from its seed; EN and RU; demo build.
